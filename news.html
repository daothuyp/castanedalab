Lab news

Papers, awards, and other milestones from the lab.

  • 2026

    Undergraduate researcher Mallory Brown awarded a Goldwater Scholarship

    Mallory, a double major in neuroscience and statistics, was recognized for her work on domesticated retrotransposon proteins in the lab and is currently helping determine the biomolecular structure of these proteins.

  • 2026

    Three new papers on ubiquilin structure and phase separation

    New work from the lab appeared in Advanced Science, Chemical Science, and The EMBO Journal this year, covering intramolecular interactions in ubiquilins, polyubiquitin-controlled phase separation, and how STI1 domains drive Dsk2 condensation. See the Publications page for details.

  • 2025

    Featured in Syracuse University Today

    The lab's research on how cells decide which damaged proteins to clear was featured in a Syracuse University story, highlighting the dual molecular- and cell-biology approach the team uses to study condensate formation.

    Our labs and offices are now located in Syracuse University's Life Sciences Complex. See the Contact page for details.
