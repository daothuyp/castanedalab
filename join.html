Open positions

We're a small, hands-on lab that combines structural biophysics with cell biology. If that mix appeals to you, here's how to reach out.

Graduate students

We welcome rotation students and are recruiting through the Biology and Chemistry graduate programs at Syracuse University. Get in touch before you apply if you'd like to talk about ongoing projects.

Postdoctoral researchers

We're happy to help develop a project and pursue funding together. Reach out with a CV and a short note about what draws you to protein quality control or condensate biology.

Undergraduate researchers

Syracuse undergraduates interested in wet-lab research are welcome to inquire, especially those who've taken (or are taking) biochemistry or cell biology.

Visiting & rotating students

If you're exploring labs for a rotation, come by — we're glad to walk through current projects and open questions.

Ready to reach out?

Send a short email introducing yourself and your interests — we read every one.
